## Specialty Crop Block Grant (detail)

- **Page:** https://www.in.gov/isda/grants-and-funding-opportunities/specialty-crop-block-grant
- Specialty crops = fruits, vegetables, tree nuts, dried fruits, horticulture, nursery (incl. floriculture). USDA list: https://www.ams.usda.gov/services/grants/scbgp/specialty-crop
- Projects must enhance competitiveness **statewide / public benefit** — not single private entity only.
- Eligible applicants (typical): for-profit, nonprofit, local gov, public/private IHEs. **Individuals alone often not eligible** — verify current NOFO.
- Contact (listed on page): Emily Conwell, Economic Development Program Director — EmConwell@isda.IN.gov
- Success story example: Slaughter Orchard & Cidery (hard cider / ground apple HACCP education project)
