# Viral craft notes (Shorts / TikTok)

## Hook formulas that fit this channel

1. **Impossible geography** — “The middle of America sat in *this* county.”
2. **Power inversion** — “A paramedic made more than the sheriff.”
3. **Ten-day first** — “Ten days after statehood…”
4. **Two senators, one tiny county**
5. **Fund jump** — “Election budget almost tripled — on the state’s own order.”

## Retention

- Strongest fact first (not chronological history dump).
- One number per beat; show it full-screen.
- Cut any fact that needs a 20-second footnote.
- Money pivot line: “Here’s the money — public record.”

## Thumbnail / first frame

- County outline + one big number OR one bold claim (≤6 words).
- Avoid seal-only thumbnails; use contrast (map pin + $).

## Music

Sparse dark rural ambient; lift on money beat; never comedy stings under SBOA language.
